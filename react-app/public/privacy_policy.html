Trackify Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

What do we do with your Information?

When you purchase something from our store, as part of the buying and selling process, we collect the personal information you give us such as your name, address and email address. When you browse our store, we also automatically receive your computer's internet protocol (IP) address in order to provide us with information that helps us learn about your browser and operating system. Email marketing (if applicable): With your permission, we may send you emails about our store, new products and other updates.

How do you get my consent?

When you provide us with personal information to complete a transaction, verify your credit card, place an order, arrange for a delivery or return a purchase, we imply that you consent to our collecting it and using it for that specific reason only. If we ask for your personal information for a secondary reason, like marketing, we will either ask you directly for your expressed consent, or provide you with an opportunity to say no.

How do I withdraw my consent?

If after you opt-in, you change your mind, you may withdraw your consent for us to contact you, for the continued collection, use or disclosure of your information, at anytime, by contacting us at support@trackify.com

How we handle location data?

We utilise your location data to run, maintain, and improve the Consumer Services, as well as to better understand you and personalise products. The most common application of location data is to determine a precise location in the event of an accident or theft.

Some Trackify Apps features and functionality require you to provide your location. When you use our Apps on your mobile device and have location services turned on, we collect and use your geocoordinates (e.g., latitude and longitude) to adapt the Consumer Services to your present location. Only with your express permission will we process your location. We will receive your device's location even if you are not using the Apps if you have persistent background location set on. Except with your approval or as permitted by this Privacy Policy, your location is never shared with anyone.

We use a variety of technologies to determine your precise location, including your operating system's or browser's location services, sensor data from your device (e.g. magnetometers, barometers, gyroscopes, accelerometers, compasses, Bluetooth data, beacon data, Wi-Fi access points, GPS data, and cell tower data), and other data that may assist us in determining your precise location.

We use the location data to send an accident alert to the emergency contacts recorded in the Trackify app in the event of an accident. This is crucial for the rider's safety and for us to be able to deliver fast assistance at that precise place. Another use of location data is to keep track of areas you've visited or parked.
