Trackify – Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) 1. What is Trackify? Answer: Trackify is a GPS tracking platform that helps you monitor the live location, route history, speed, ignition status, geofences, and other vehicle information in real time. 2. Which vehicles are supported by Trackify? Answer: Trackify supports GPS devices installed in bikes, cars, trucks, buses, commercial vehicles, and other GPS-enabled assets. 3. How do I view my vehicle's live location? Answer: Log in to your Trackify account and open the Map page. Your vehicle's current location will be displayed on the map. 4. Why is my vehicle showing offline? Answer: A vehicle may appear offline due to: No GSM/mobile network Device power disconnected SIM card issue Vehicle battery disconnected GPS device malfunction 5. Why is my vehicle location not updating? Answer: Possible reasons include poor GPS signal, weak mobile network, or the GPS device being powered off. 6. How can I check my trip history? Answer: Go to the History section, select your vehicle and date range, then generate the report. 7. What is Geofence? Answer: A Geofence is a virtual boundary. Trackify sends alerts whenever a vehicle enters or exits the defined area. 8. How do I create a Geofence? Answer: Navigate to the Geofence page, draw the required area on the map, enter a name, and save it. 9. What should I do if the GPS device is not sending data? Answer: Check the following: Device power connection SIM card validity and data plan GPS antenna GSM network availability If the issue persists, contact the administrator or support team. 10. Why is my vehicle's location incorrect? Answer: Incorrect location may occur due to weak GPS signal, underground parking, tunnels, or poor satellite reception. 11. Can I track multiple vehicles? Answer: Yes. Trackify allows you to monitor multiple vehicles from a single dashboard. 12. Can I receive overspeed alerts? Answer: Yes. You can configure overspeed alerts in the notification settings. 13. How do I assign a GPS device to a vehicle? Answer: Go to the Device Assignment page, select the user and vehicle, choose the IMEI/device, and click Assign. 14. How can I contact Trackify Support? Answer: If you experience any issues, please contact your Trackify administrator or the support team.
